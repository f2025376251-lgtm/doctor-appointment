EMAIL SETTINGS

After a booking is confirmed, the patient receives a confirmation email and the selected doctor receives an HTML table of the appointment details. Add a Gmail App Password once.

Gmail (for sending email)

Use the doctor Gmail, turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an App Password at Google Account → Security → App passwords. Paste the 16-character password here.

Supabase (optional)

Appointments are always saved locally. If you add Supabase, they are also stored in your appointments table.
